# Reader first

**Rule.** Line 1 is the answer. Everything after it earns its place. Detail lives in files the reader can open when they want it.

**Why.** Long agent replies cause fatigue. The human skims, misses the one line that mattered, and trusts less.

**Bad.** Four paragraphs recapping the request, the files read, and the approach, with the result in paragraph five.

**Good.** "Rate limiting works: 101st request → 429 (EVIDENCE C1). One gate: push the branch? Details: .flow/tasks/rate-limit/TRACE.md"

**Check yourself.** If the reader reads only your first line, do they get what they need?
