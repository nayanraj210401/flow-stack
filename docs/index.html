The problem

Agents say done too early.

Without guardrails, you find these problems in review, which is the most expensive place to find them. flow-stack catches them while the agent is still working.

Plain agent

  • ×Starts coding from a vague prompt and guesses what "done" means.
  • ×Weakens a failing test until it goes green.
  • ×Writes a test that passes even with the change reverted.
  • ×Adds a new helper next to one that already exists.
  • ×Retries the same broken fix five times.
  • ×Says "Done!" with nothing run since the last edit.

With flow-stack

  • ✓Writes INTENT.md with runnable checks. You approve it once.
  • ✓Seals the checks. Changing one needs your sign-off.
  • ✓Probes each check: revert the source, and the check must fail.
  • ✓Looks for code to delete, reuse, or configure before adding any.
  • ✓Circuit breaker: after 3 identical failures, it rethinks the approach.
  • ✓A hook sends "done" back unless a check passed after the last edit.
How it works

Mostly, you just talk.

Questions get a short, cited answer, and one-line fixes get made and checked. Features, bugs, refactors, and optimizations go through a playbook that asks for you only at the gates.

01

Understand

Repo map, how and why, and the features the change touches.

GATE
02

Intent

Goal, non-goals, and runnable checks, then sealed.

03

Challenge

A blind advocate designs it too. The do-nothing option is always on the table.

04

Slice

Small steps, each with a check, a file fence, and a line budget.

05

Loop

Red, subtract, build, verify, probe, proof gate.

06

Prove

Blind checks, review, deslop, and the debt ledger.

GATE
07

Present & merge

A risk-ranked tour of the diff and tagged claims, then you approve the merge.

GATE
08

Close

Trace, reflect, and profile updates for you to approve.

The inner loop runs once per slice.

  1. RedRun the check first and watch it fail, so you know it can catch the problem.
  2. SubtractDelete, reuse, or configure before writing anything new.
  3. BuildThe smallest change, inside the slice's file fence.
  4. Verify + probePass through evidence.sh, then revert the source: the check must fail.
  5. Proof gateOnly task.sh slice done can mark a slice finished, and only with red, green, and probe passed and the line budget kept.

Autonomous mode

Go to bed. Wake up to a queue.

Put --auto anywhere in a prompt and the hooks hold the agent to it. Saying "going to bed" or "run until done" switches the agent to the same behavior, without the hook enforcement. The agent stops asking you questions. It takes the reversible decisions itself and queues the irreversible ones for your return.

--auto

Approves its own intent and seals, takes its recommended option at every reversible gate, and logs each choice as who=agent. Hooks deny questions, push, PR, and publish commands, and the agent queues them instead.

--auto ship

Same as --auto, but the agent may also push its branch (never main), open the PR, and merge it once ready.sh has stamped HEAD after review. Deploys and publishing still wait for you.

--no-auto

You're back, and gates ask you again. Approve or reject what's queued from /flow-pane, or read it in the handoff.

Unless you grant --auto ship, irreversible actions wait for you. Hooks block push, PR, and publish commands; a deploy script is on the agent to hold back. Each queued item waits in GATES.md with its options and a recommendation. At the end, the agent notifies you and writes a trace and a handoff that lists the open gates first.

Every repo, one screen.

Needs you

Open gates, blocked slices, tasks ready to close, PRs waiting on your review, and stale features, from every repo, at the top.

Quality per repo

A 0–100 score from features verified, checks that pass and have teeth, open debt, the review stamp on HEAD, and test files per source file. No data shows n/a, never 0.

Everything else

Open tasks and their slices, recent agent decisions, the debt ledger, shipped work, and spend from ccusage. Custom panels are jq over the same data.

The board is a mockup and the commands are a demo. The real board is a private claude.ai Artifact. It shows repo names, PR titles, commit subjects, and debt and decision text, so check it before you share the link.

Guardrails

Hooks on every tool call. Zero tokens.

They're bash and jq, and they run whether or not a skill is loaded. No rigor setting turns them off.

guard Bash

Denies destructive commands, reads of secrets, and packages that don't exist on npm or PyPI. Asks before risky commands.

$ cat .env denied · secret read

seal Edit · Write

Approved checks are hashed. Weakening one to get a pass needs your confirmation.

edit tests/rate.test.ts sealed · ask the human

fence Edit · Write

Edits outside the slice's files are denied until the fence is widened deliberately and logged.

edit src/billing/invoice.ts outside fence · S2 owns src/limit/

claims Stop

"Done", "works", or "fixed" with no passing check since the last edit gets sent back.

"All done!" no evidence · verify or say ~ assumed

circuit PostToolUse

After the same failing check three times, the agent stops, rethinks its approach, and asks you.

C2 FAIL ×3 · attack the premise

holds Edit · Write

When two sessions edit the same file, the second is told who holds it and pointed at SendMessage.

Fits your setup

It learns your repo and your taste.

Your profile, your rules

~/.flow-stack/profile.md holds your role, hard rules, taste, rigor, and model budget. reflect turns repeated corrections into proposed updates, and nothing changes until you approve.

Forge: skills specific to your repo

Generic skills have slots. Forge skills fill them for your repo, and tend re-runs their self-tests to catch drift.

make-verifierdrive your app like a user make-runnerbuild, run, reset make-gatesyour repo's irreversible actions make-playbookyour workflows

Feature map

One file per user-facing feature. features.sh impact lists the features your diff touches, and their scenarios become part of "done".

Agent hosts

In herdr, Orca, and cmux, the host's sidebar shows the task, slice, and open gates. In herdr, you can opt in to run workers in panes you can watch.

Resume cold

Intent, evidence, decisions, and the trail live in .flow/tasks/. A fresh session picks up with /flow resume.
